Jaali: Lattice Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

What the game keeps

The game keeps your best score, your daily streak, the date of your last daily puzzle and your sound setting. It keeps these only on your own device, in your browser storage. They are not sent to us.

What the game does not collect

The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. It has no account and no sign-in. We do not sell any data.

Ads and game portals

If you play the game on a game portal (for example CrazyGames) or in a version with ads, that portal or ad provider can show ads and can collect data under its own privacy policy. Please read the policy of that portal or provider.

Children

The game is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Delete your data

To delete all game data, clear the site data for this game in your browser settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
